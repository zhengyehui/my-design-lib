Craftsmanship

The Pursuit of Perfection

Every Meridian timepiece passes through 47 quality control checkpoints before earning the right to bear our name.

Movement Assembly

Each caliber is hand-assembled from 312 individual components by a single master watchmaker over 120 hours.

Case Finishing

Alternating brushed and polished surfaces create a play of light that transforms with every movement of the wrist.

Guilloché Dial

Engine-turned dials with intricate patterns created on century-old rose engines, each requiring 40 hours of precision work.

COSC Certification

Every movement undergoes 15 days of rigorous testing across 5 positions and 3 temperatures to achieve chronometer status.
