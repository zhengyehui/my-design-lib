Message from Leadership

A Year of
Transformative
Growth

In 2025, Meridian Group reached a defining inflection point in our three-decade journey. Against a backdrop of accelerating climate urgency and evolving global regulation, we demonstrated that financial performance and environmental stewardship are not competing objectives — they are inseparable forces that drive long-term value creation.

Our integrated sustainability strategy, launched in 2020 under the Meridian Horizon framework, has matured into a fully embedded operating model. Every business unit, every capital allocation decision, and every partnership now passes through the lens of our triple-bottom-line commitment: planet, people, and prosperity.

This report details our progress against the ambitious targets we set five years ago — and introduces the next generation of commitments that will guide us through 2030 and beyond. We are transparent about where we have exceeded expectations and honest about where challenges remain.

As the world navigates the complexities of the energy transition, supply chain resilience, and social equity, Meridian Group stands ready to lead — not through rhetoric, but through measurable action, accountable governance, and an unwavering belief that business must be a force for regenerative impact.

Letter from the Chief Executive Officer
Sustainability is not a department, a line item, or a marketing narrative. It is the architecture upon which every successful enterprise of the next century will be built.
EV

Elena Vasquez

Chief Executive Officer, Meridian Group

Dear shareholders, partners, and stakeholders,

When I assumed the role of CEO in 2019, I made a commitment that Meridian Group would not simply adapt to the sustainability transition — we would lead it. Six years later, I am proud to report that our organization has fundamentally transformed how we create, deliver, and measure value.

In 2025, we achieved record financial performance while simultaneously deepening our environmental commitments. Revenue reached $4.2 billion, a 19% increase from the prior year, with our clean energy and circular economy divisions contributing more than half of total revenue for the first time in our history. Our EBITDA margin expanded to 23.4%, demonstrating that our sustainability investments are generating tangible financial returns.

Perhaps most significantly, we reduced our combined Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 42% against our 2019 baseline — exceeding our interim Science-Based Target two years ahead of schedule. This achievement was driven by our accelerated coal phase-out in Southeast Asia, the completion of our European renewable energy portfolio, and breakthrough efficiency gains in our manufacturing operations across North America.

But numbers alone do not tell the full story. This year, we launched the Meridian Regenerative Fund, a $200 million commitment to restore degraded ecosystems in the communities where we operate. We welcomed 3,200 new team members, with a particular focus on diversity in STEM roles. And we deepened our engagement with indigenous communities in our supply chain, establishing co-governance models that respect traditional land rights while advancing sustainable resource management.

Looking ahead, the challenge is immense but so is our conviction. The climate crisis demands urgency. Social inequality demands justice. And our stakeholders — from institutional investors to frontline communities — demand accountability. I assure you that Meridian Group will continue to meet these demands with transparency, ambition, and action.

With gratitude and determination,

Elena Vasquez

Our Framework

Three Pillars of
Sustainable Value

🌿

Environmental

Our environmental stewardship extends beyond compliance to active regeneration. Through science-based targets and nature-positive investments, we are working to restore the ecosystems upon which all life depends.

  • Achieved carbon neutrality in Scope 1 & 2 emissions across all European operations, with a verified pathway to group-wide neutrality by 2028
  • Invested $340 million in biodiversity restoration projects spanning 12 countries, including the Meridian Amazon Reforestation Initiative
  • Reduced freshwater withdrawal intensity by 28% through closed-loop water systems implemented across 34 manufacturing facilities
  • Eliminated 94% of single-use plastics from our product packaging, with full elimination targeted for Q2 2026
👥

Social

People are the foundation of everything we build. Our social impact strategy centers on equity, inclusion, community empowerment, and the dignity of every worker in our value chain.

  • Achieved gender pay parity across all 38 countries of operation, verified by independent third-party audit conducted by Equileap
  • Invested $85 million in community development programs, reaching 2.3 million beneficiaries in education, healthcare, and economic opportunity
  • Established the Meridian Skills Academy, providing free STEM education and vocational training to 15,000 young people in underserved communities
⚖️

Governance

Ethical governance is the bedrock of trust. Our board-level sustainability oversight, transparent reporting, and stakeholder engagement practices set the standard for corporate accountability.

  • Board composition now includes 50% independent directors with dedicated ESG expertise, exceeding FTSE Russell guidelines for board diversity
  • Published our first Task Force on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TNFD) report, joining fewer than 200 companies globally to adopt this framework
  • Implemented AI-powered supply chain monitoring across 100% of Tier 1 and 82% of Tier 2 suppliers, with zero tolerance for forced labor or environmental violations
  • Tied 30% of executive compensation to measurable ESG outcomes, including emissions reduction, diversity targets, and safety performance
Our Commitment in Action

Measurable Impact

0M

Trees Planted

Through the Meridian Reforestation Alliance, we planted 2.1 million native trees across degraded landscapes in Brazil, Indonesia, and the Congo Basin. Each project is co-designed with local communities and monitored using satellite imagery and blockchain-verified carbon credits.

100%

Renewable Energy by 2030

We are on track to power 100% of our global operations with renewable energy by 2030. Currently at 78% — up from 52% in 2022 — our renewable portfolio includes wind, solar, and geothermal assets across four continents, with 1.2 GW of installed capacity.

Zero

Waste to Landfill

Twenty-two of our 41 global facilities now achieve zero waste to landfill status. Across the group, we have diverted 97% of operational waste from landfills through circular design, material recovery, and innovative upcycling partnerships with local enterprises.

Meridian Horizon Strategic Plan

Strategic Outlook

Meridian Horizon
2030

In 2024, our Board of Directors approved the next evolution of our sustainability strategy — Meridian Horizon 2030. This comprehensive framework establishes 47 measurable targets across environmental, social, and governance dimensions, aligned with the Paris Agreement, the UN Sustainable Development Goals, and the Global Biodiversity Framework.

Central to this strategy is our commitment to achieving net-zero value chain emissions by 2040 — a full decade ahead of the Paris Agreement's 2050 timeline. To reach this goal, we are investing $3.5 billion over the next five years in clean energy infrastructure, green hydrogen development, carbon capture technology, and the electrification of our global logistics network.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

Q1 2025

Meridian Regenerative Fund Launch

Announced a $200 million fund dedicated to ecosystem restoration and biodiversity projects. Initial investments targeted mangrove restoration in Southeast Asia and soil regeneration in sub-Saharan Africa, partnering with the World Wildlife Fund and local community cooperatives.

Q2 2025

First TNFD Report Published

Became one of fewer than 200 companies worldwide to publish a comprehensive report aligned with the Task Force on Nature-related Financial Disclosures, mapping our dependencies and impacts across 14 priority ecosystems and establishing nature-related risk assessments for all major capital projects.

Q3 2025

Green Hydrogen Partnership with HyVelocity

Signed a landmark $480 million joint venture with HyVelocity Energy to develop green hydrogen production facilities in Northern Europe and the Gulf Coast of the United States, targeting 500 MW of electrolyzer capacity by 2028.

Q4 2025

42% Carbon Reduction Milestone Achieved

Exceeded our interim Science-Based Target two years ahead of schedule, driven by the completion of our European coal phase-out, the commissioning of the Meridian Solar Array in the Mojave Desert, and a 31% improvement in manufacturing energy efficiency across our Asia-Pacific operations.

Ongoing

Skills Academy Global Expansion

Expanded the Meridian Skills Academy to 12 countries, providing free STEM education, digital literacy, and vocational training to 15,000 young people. In partnership with UNESCO and local education ministries, the program focuses on empowering girls and young women in communities transitioning from fossil fuel-dependent economies.
