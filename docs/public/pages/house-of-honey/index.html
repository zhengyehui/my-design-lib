Our Studio

House of Honey is a Los Angeles-based interior design studio founded in 2018. We specialize in creating warm, refined spaces that balance contemporary sensibility with timeless elegance. Our approach centers on the belief that every space should tell a story — your story.

From concept to completion, we work closely with our clients to bring their vision to life, drawing inspiration from the natural beauty of California living, global design traditions, and the unique character of each project. Every material, color, and detail is carefully considered to create environments that feel both aspirational and deeply personal.
