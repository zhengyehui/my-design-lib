Our Story
From Vine to Glass

Domaine Cuvée Noir

Burgundy, France · Since 1947

For three generations, our family has tended the ancient vines of Burgundy, nurturing each grape with the patience and reverence that only time can teach. Every bottle tells the story of a specific moment in the life of our terroir.

Our winemakers practice minimal intervention, allowing the land to speak through each vintage. The result is wines of extraordinary complexity — each sip a conversation between earth, sky, and the hands that guided them.
