Our Philosophy

The art of honoring what is fleeting

Kaiseki is more than cuisine — it is a meditation on impermanence. Each plate is composed to reflect the moment: the first cherry blossom of spring, the last crimson maple of autumn. We source exclusively from Kyoto's morning markets and our own garden, ensuring every ingredient speaks of its season.

The Space

An intimate sanctuary of stillness

Our counter, carved from a single piece of hinoki cypress, seats only eight guests. The space is designed around the concept of "ma" — the beauty of negative space — where what is absent is as important as what is present.

  • Hand-planed hinoki counter from Yoshino
  • Seasonal ikebana arrangements
  • Private garden view with moss and stone
  • Sake collection of 40+ labels
  • Traditional tatami private room (4 guests)
