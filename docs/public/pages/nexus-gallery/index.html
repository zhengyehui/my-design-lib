The Visionaries

Featured Artists

Six pioneering artists working at the intersection of code, creativity, and consciousness. Their diverse practices span generative art, machine learning, and immersive digital environments.

Elena Vasquez

Generative Artist

Madrid-born, Berlin-based. Elena's work explores emergent complexity through algorithmic systems, creating vast cosmic landscapes from simple mathematical rules.

3 works in exhibition

Kenji Murakami

AI Artist & Researcher

Tokyo-based researcher and artist pushing the boundaries of human-AI creative collaboration. His work questions authorship in the age of machine intelligence.

2 works in exhibition

Zara Okonkwo

Digital Sculptor

Lagos and London-based. Zara transforms data into physical and digital sculptures, revealing hidden patterns in human migration, language, and cultural exchange.

2 works in exhibition

Liam Chen

Creative Coder

Vancouver-based creative technologist whose procedural ecosystems blur the line between digital nature and computational imagination. Former Pixar technical director.

2 works in exhibition

Anya Petrov

Data Artist

Moscow-born, Amsterdam-based. Anya transforms scientific datasets into haunting visual meditations on climate, memory, and the fragility of our digital infrastructure.

1 work in exhibition

Samuel Osei

Immersive Designer

Accra-based immersive experience designer creating large-scale digital environments that respond to human presence, breath, and collective movement.

2 works in exhibition

Exhibition Programme

Schedule

A carefully curated programme of openings, artist talks, workshops, and immersive experiences running throughout the exhibition period.

September 15, 2026

Grand Opening & Reception

Join us for the inaugural evening featuring live generative art performances, artist meet-and-greet, and a champagne reception in the main gallery hall.

October 1 — 3, 2026

Artist Talk Series: Code as Canvas

Three-day intensive with Elena Vasquez, Kenji Murakami, and Liam Chen exploring the philosophy and practice of computational art creation.

October 18, 2026

Workshop: Generative Art Fundamentals

A hands-on workshop led by Liam Chen introducing participants to creative coding with p5.js and algorithmic design principles. All skill levels welcome.

November 5 — 12, 2026

Immersive Week: Northern Algorithm

Experience Samuel Osei's full-room immersive installation in an expanded format with synchronized sound design and responsive lighting environments.

November 22, 2026

Panel: AI, Art & Authorship

A thought-provoking panel discussion featuring all six exhibiting artists, moderated by digital art critic Maya Hernandez. Exploring the future of creative AI.

December 30, 2026

Closing Night: Lumina Finale

The exhibition culminates in an extraordinary closing ceremony featuring a collaborative generative artwork created live by all six artists simultaneously.
