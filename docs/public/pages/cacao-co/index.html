Flavor Profiles

The Language of Taste

Like wine, fine chocolate reveals layers of flavor. Here's what you might discover in each bar.

🌸

Floral

Jasmine, lavender, and orange blossom — found in high-altitude Ecuadorian Nacional beans

🍒

Fruity

Cherry, raspberry, and tropical mango — characteristic of Madagascar and Peruvian origins

🌰

Nutty

Almond, hazelnut, and toasted sesame — emerging during our slow-roast process

🍯

Caramel

Brown sugar, honey, and butterscotch — developed during extended conching time
