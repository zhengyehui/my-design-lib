About the Studio

We shape spaces that
define silence

Atelier Noir is a multi-disciplinary architecture and interior design studio founded on the belief that restraint is the highest form of expression. We create spaces where material, light, and proportion speak without excess.

Our practice spans residential, commercial, and cultural projects across four continents, guided by a philosophy of quiet permanence over fleeting trends.
