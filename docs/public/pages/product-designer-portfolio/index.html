About

Designing with intention,
building with care

I'm Maren Vogt, a product designer and creative director based in Berlin, Germany. With over a decade of experience spanning startups and global enterprises, I specialize in translating complex challenges into elegant, human-centered solutions.

My approach blends strategic thinking with meticulous craft. I believe the best products emerge from deep empathy with users, a rigorous design process, and the courage to simplify what others overcomplicate. Every pixel, every interaction, every moment of delight — it all matters.

Before founding my independent practice, I led design teams at Figma and Spotify, where I helped shape products used by millions worldwide. Today, I partner with forward-thinking companies to create digital experiences that genuinely improve people's lives.

"Great design isn't about what you add — it's about what you have the courage to leave out."
