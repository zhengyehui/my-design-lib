About Forma

Where craft meets
cinematic vision

Forma is a motion and 3D design studio founded on the belief that great visual work exists at the intersection of artistry and precision. We collaborate with forward-thinking brands to create motion identities, immersive 3D worlds, and digital experiences that leave lasting impressions.

Based in London and working globally, our multidisciplinary team brings together directors, designers, and technologists who share a passion for pushing visual boundaries.
