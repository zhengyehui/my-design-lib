DESIGN IS NOT WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE.
DESIGN IS HOW IT WORKS.

Typography is the voice of design. It whispers, shouts, sings, and silences. Every curve of a letterform carries intention. Every kerning pair is a conversation between characters — a negotiation of space that determines how meaning lands.

The space between letters is not empty. It is full of tension, rhythm, and breath. To design type is to choreograph silence.

— The Manifesto, 2025
