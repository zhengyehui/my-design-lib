Our Philosophy

We believe that great sake is not made — it is revealed. Like the wind that shapes the mountains over millennia, we guide the rice and water with patience, allowing nature to express its deepest character through every bottle.

The Process

From Rice to Revelation

01
洗
Washing
Each grain of Yamada Nishiki rice is washed by hand with the precision of a master calligrapher, removing impurities while preserving the starchy heart.
02
蒸
Steaming
Steamed over mountain spring water in traditional koshiki steamers. The rice emerges with a perfect outer firmness and tender center.
03
麹
Koji
In the koji room, maintained at precise temperatures, Aspergillus oryzae transforms starch into sugar over 48 hours of careful cultivation.
04
醸
Brewing
The triple-addition method, or sandan shikomi, unfolds over four days. Fermentation continues slowly through the cold Niigata winter.

Terroir

Where Mountain
Winds Are Born

Our brewery sits at the foot of Mount Myoko in Niigata Prefecture, where winter winds carry moisture from the Sea of Japan, blanketing the region in meters of pristine snow.

This snowmelt filters through volcanic rock over decades, emerging as some of the softest, purest water in Japan — the soul of every bottle we produce.

"The sake remembers every snowflake that melted into the stream that feeds our wells."
