Brewing Guide

The Perfect
Cup, Every Time

Japanese tea is an art. Follow our guide to unlock the full potential of every leaf.

01
💧

Heat Water

Use filtered water heated to the right temperature. Each tea has its ideal range.

60°C – 80°C
02
⚖️

Measure Leaves

Use 2-3g of tea per 200ml of water. For matcha, use 1-2g with 70ml water.

2-3g per cup
03
⏳

Steep Perfectly

Green teas need just 60-90 seconds. Over-steeping releases bitter tannins.

60 – 90 sec
04
🍵

Savor & Enjoy

Drink mindfully. Notice the color, aroma, and evolving flavors with each sip.

Multiple infusions
