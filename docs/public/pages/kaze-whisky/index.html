Heritage

A Century of Mastery

1923 — Founding
The First Distillery in Nagano
Master distiller Takeshi Kaze established the distillery at the foot of the Japanese Alps, drawn by the pristine water and extreme temperature variations that would define our signature maturation process.
1967 — Innovation
The Mizunara Discovery
Third-generation master Kenji Kaze pioneered the use of Japanese Mizunara oak for whisky aging — a decision that would take decades to bear fruit but ultimately define Kaze's unmistakable character.
The Craft

From Grain to Glass

01
Selection
Hand-selected barley from Hokkaido farms. Each harvest is tasted and graded before malting begins.
02
Distillation
Slow distillation in copper pot stills. The heart cut is taken at a precise moment — determined by aroma alone.
03
Maturation
Rested in our mountain warehouses where -15°C winters and 35°C summers create an angel's share unlike anywhere else on earth.
