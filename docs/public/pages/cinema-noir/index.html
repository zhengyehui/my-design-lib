Behind the Lens

The Art of
Chiaroscuro

Born from German Expressionism and forged in the streets of post-war Hollywood, film noir transformed cinema into a canvas of moral shadows. High-contrast lighting, Dutch angles, and rain-slicked streets became the visual language of disillusionment.

Today, FRAME preserves and reimagines this aesthetic for the digital age — where every pixel can cast a shadow, and every interaction tells a story.
