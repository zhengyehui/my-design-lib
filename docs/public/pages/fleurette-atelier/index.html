Our Story

Rooted in nature, guided by artistry

Founded by floral artist Camille Beaumont in 2014, Fleurette grew from a small Marais studio into one of Paris's most sought-after floral ateliers. Our philosophy is simple: every arrangement should feel like it was just gathered from a secret garden.

We source seasonally from French growers and forage from the countryside surrounding Paris, ensuring each creation carries the authentic beauty of the moment.
