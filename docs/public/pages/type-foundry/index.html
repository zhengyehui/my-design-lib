About the Foundry

We design type
for brands that
dare to speak
loudly.

Mono Type Foundry is an independent type design studio based in Barcelona. We create custom typefaces and retail fonts for brands, publishers, and digital products that demand a distinctive voice.

Our philosophy is simple: every letterform carries emotion. A well-crafted typeface doesn't just convey words — it conveys intention, personality, and trust. We believe typography is the silent ambassador of design.

From editorial serif families to variable display faces, our work spans the full spectrum of contemporary type design. Each project begins with research and ends with pixels — and occasionally, lead.
