GOOD TO KNOW

BAGS, CHANGES, BOARDING — THE HONEST ANSWERS

EVERY FARE INCLUDES ONE CABIN BAG OF UP TO 8 KG AND ONE SMALL PERSONAL ITEM THAT FITS UNDER THE SEAT IN FRONT OF YOU. HOLD BAGGAGE DEPENDS ON YOUR CABIN — 23 KG IN ECONOMY, 2 × 23 KG IN PREMIUM AND 3 × 23 KG IN BUSINESS. SPORTS EQUIPMENT COUNTS AS A HOLD BAG IF IT STAYS WITHIN 23 KG AND 190 CM LINEAR.

YES. DATE AND TIME CHANGES ARE FREE ON PREMIUM AND BUSINESS FARES AND CHARGED AT €39 ON ECONOMY, UP TO 24 HOURS BEFORE DEPARTURE. CANCELLATIONS WITHIN 24 HOURS OF BOOKING ARE ALWAYS FULLY REFUNDABLE. AFTER THAT, ECONOMY FARES REFUND TO TRAVEL CREDIT VALID FOR 24 MONTHS, WHILE PREMIUM AND BUSINESS FARES REFUND TO THE ORIGINAL PAYMENT METHOD.

ONLINE CHECK-IN OPENS 30 HOURS BEFORE DEPARTURE AND CLOSES 60 MINUTES BEFORE. AT THE AIRPORT, BAG DROP CLOSES 40 MINUTES BEFORE DEPARTURE ON SCHENGEN ROUTES AND 60 MINUTES ON NON-SCHENGEN ROUTES. BOARDING CLOSES 20 MINUTES BEFORE DEPARTURE — OUR GATES SHARPEN ON TIME, EVERY TIME.

ON EVERY FLIGHT OVER 75 MINUTES WE SERVE A COMPLIMENTARY COLD NORDIC PLATE IN ECONOMY — OPEN SANDWICH, SEASONAL SALAD AND A SWEET. PREMIUM ADDS A WARM SMÖRGÅS AND A FULL BEVERAGE CART, AND BUSINESS FEATURES A CHEF-DESIGNED MENU WITH A CHAMPAGNE SERVICE. ALLERGEN INFORMATION IS AVAILABLE IN THE APP 48 HOURS BEFORE DEPARTURE.

IF WE DELAY YOU BY MORE THAN 90 MINUTES YOU GET MEAL VOUCHERS AND FREE WI-FI IMMEDIATELY, NO QUESTIONS ASKED. FOR CANCELLATIONS WE REBOOK YOU ON THE NEXT AVAILABLE CIRRUS SERVICE — OR A PARTNER AIRLINE IF IT GETS YOU THERE SOONER — AND COVER MEALS AND HOTEL STAYS WHEN AN OVERNIGHT IS UNAVOIDABLE. EU261 COMPENSATION APPLIES WHERE ELIGIBLE.
