What I Do

🎨

3D Modeling & Texturing

High-poly and low-poly modeling, UV unwrapping, PBR texturing for product visualization, characters, and environments.

🎬

Motion Design

Character animation, product reveals, abstract motion graphics, and audio-reactive visuals for brand campaigns and live events.

✨

Look Development

Shading, lighting, and compositing to achieve photorealistic or stylized aesthetics. Expert in Octane, V-Ray, and Redshift.

Real-Time & WebGL

Architectural Viz

Product Visualization

Workflow

How I Work

A structured creative process that ensures every project delivers on both vision and quality.

Discovery

Understanding your vision, brand, and technical requirements. Mood boards and reference gathering.

Concept

Sketches, styleframes, and previz. Defining the visual direction before production begins.

Production

Modeling, texturing, animation, and rendering. Regular check-ins with work-in-progress renders.

Delivery

Final compositing, color grading, and format delivery. All source files included.
