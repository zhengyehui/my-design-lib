Notre Histoire

The Art of Darkness

Every NOIR Parfum creation begins in the amber-lit ateliers of Grasse, where master perfumers compose in whispers. We source only ingredients that have been touched by time — oud resin aged for decades, iris pallida harvested after three years of patient cultivation, and jasmine picked by hand in the fleeting hours before dawn.

"A great perfume is not worn — it is inhabited. It becomes the space between you and the world."

Our philosophy is one of restraint and revelation. Each fragrance is built around a single precious ingredient, then elaborated with layers that unfold over hours. The result is not merely a scent, but an atmosphere — a private world of shadow and light that exists only on skin.

Les Ingrédients

Precious Materials

Sourced from the world's most revered terroirs — each ingredient tells its own story.

Rose de Mai

Grasse, France

Hand-picked at dawn in the fields of Grasse. 5,000 blooms yield a single gram of absolute — honeyed, velvety, eternal.

Oud d'Assam

Assam, India

Wild agarwood aged for over twenty years. Smoky, animalic, sacred — the most prized material in perfumery.

Jasmine de Nuit

Grasse, France

Night-harvested jasmine sambac. Its narcotic sweetness deepens after midnight — indolic, luminous, unforgettable.

Vetiver Bourbon

Réunion Island

Volcanic vetiver from Réunion. Earthy, smoky, with a cool mineral edge that grounds every composition.

Bergamotte de Calabre

Calabria, Italy

Cold-pressed from the rind of sun-ripened bergamot. Sparkling, bitter, with an aristocratic green elegance.

Ambre Gris

Atlantic Ocean

A rare oceanic treasure — saline, musky, warm. Adds an iridescent quality that makes every scent shimmer.

Artisanal Craft

Each fragrance is composed by hand, tested over 18 months, and refined through 200 iterations before release.

Rare Origins

We source from six continents. Every ingredient is traceable, ethically harvested, and selected for its singular character.

Timeless Design

Our bottles are objects of beauty — hand-blown glass, weighted caps, and labels letterpress-printed on cotton rag paper.
