About

Stories told through texture, colour & quiet gesture.

Based in Lyon, France, I create illustrations that live at the intersection of warmth and whimsy. My work draws from natural forms, vintage palettes, and the small beauty found in everyday moments.

With over a decade of experience spanning editorial, packaging, and gallery work, I partner with brands and publishers who value craft, narrative, and a human touch.
