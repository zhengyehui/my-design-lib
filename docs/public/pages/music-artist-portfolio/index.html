The journey so far
2025
Released "Neon Horizons" to critical acclaim. Sold-out world tour spanning 40 cities. Headlined Berghain's legendary NYE marathon set.
2023
Third album "Pulse Memory" debuted at #3 on the Electronic charts. Collaborated with modular synth pioneer Suzanne Ciani on the single "Crystal Lattice."
2020
"Static Dreams" explored ambient territories during lockdown. Livestreamed a 4-hour set from an empty Berghain to 500,000 viewers worldwide.
2017
Debut album "Origin Signal" released on Kompakt Records. Named "Breakthrough Artist" by Resident Advisor. First international tour.
2012
