Our Story

Born in the Mountains, Built for the Trail

Terrain was founded in 2019 by a group of alpine guides who were tired of gear that couldn't keep up. Every product we make is designed in our Chamonix studio and tested on the world's toughest terrain.

We believe the best outdoor gear should be invisible — so you can focus on what matters: the summit, the trail, the moment.
