The Playform method

Three rules we
never break

We treat a toddler's toy with the same rigor as a chair for a design museum. Here is what that means in practice.

01

Materials

Solid European beech from FSC-certified forests, coloured with water-based pigments and sealed with linseed oil. No MDF, no plastic, no synthetic fragrances — only what a forest would recognise.

02

Safety

Every edge is radiused to 0.4 mm and every piece passes the EN71-3 mouthing test before it leaves Copenhagen. Small parts are measured, weighed and drop-tested 2,000 times per batch.

03

Imagination

A good toy does not explain itself. We design open-ended shapes with no screens, no sounds and no single right answer — so the story stays in the child's hands.
