Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before making your decision.

For small teams, FlowDesk Pro offers the best value with its intuitive interface and lower per-seat pricing. If your team is developer-heavy, Arc Platform might be a better fit due to its CLI-first approach and excellent API documentation. All three platforms offer free tiers, so we recommend testing each with your team before committing.
Yes! Nexus Suite offers built-in migration tools for FlowDesk, Arc, Asana, Monday, and Jira. Arc Platform provides a comprehensive migration API with detailed documentation. FlowDesk Pro supports CSV imports and has a dedicated migration team for enterprise customers. Most migrations complete within 1-3 business days.
FlowDesk Pro focuses on AI-assisted design — auto-generating layouts, suggesting color palettes, and smart asset organization. Nexus Suite offers the most comprehensive AI with natural language queries, automated reporting, content generation, and predictive analytics. Arc Platform provides AI code completion and smart workflow suggestions but is more limited in scope. All use GPT-4 class models.
Absolutely. FlowDesk Pro offers a 14-day free trial with full features. Nexus Suite provides a generous 30-day trial with no credit card required. Arc Platform has a permanent free tier for up to 5 users with limited storage, plus a 14-day trial of all pro features. We recommend starting with Nexus Suite's trial due to its length and zero-friction signup.
All three platforms are SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Nexus Suite and Arc Platform both offer SSO/SAML on all paid plans, while FlowDesk restricts it to Enterprise. For industries like healthcare or finance, Nexus Suite has the most comprehensive compliance package including HIPAA BAA, ISO 27001, and FedRAMP authorization. Arc Platform leads in penetration testing transparency with public reports.
Arc Platform has the best offline support with a full desktop app that syncs when you reconnect. FlowDesk Pro offers limited offline editing in its desktop app. Nexus Suite requires an active internet connection for most features, though you can cache documents for viewing. If offline access is critical, Arc Platform is the clear winner.
