🐾 Our Story

Welcome to Whiskers & Brew

A cozy corner where artisan coffee meets feline friendship

More Than Just a Coffee Shop

Founded in 2020, Whiskers & Brew began with a simple dream: create a warm, welcoming space where people could enjoy exceptional coffee while spending time with rescue cats looking for their forever homes.

Every cat in our café is a rescue, and every visit supports our mission to find them loving families. Whether you're here for a latte, a cuddle, or both — you're making a difference.
