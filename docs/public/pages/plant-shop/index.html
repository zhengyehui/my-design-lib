Plant Care 101

Keep Your Plants Thriving

Simple tips to help your green friends flourish in any environment.

Right Light, Right Spot

Match your plant to the light conditions in your space. Bright indirect light works for most tropical plants, while low-light varieties thrive in shadier corners.

Water Wisely

Overwatering is the most common mistake. Always check the top inch of soil — if it's dry, it's time to water. Most plants prefer a thorough soak over frequent sips.

Keep It Comfortable

Most houseplants prefer temperatures between 65–80°F (18–27°C). Avoid placing them near cold drafts, heating vents, or air conditioning units.
