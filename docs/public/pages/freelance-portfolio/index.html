About

The Visionary Behind the Lens

Lena Voss is an award-winning cinematographer based in Los Angeles, with a passion for translating emotion into visual language. Over the past eight years, she has lensed features, documentaries, and commercial projects across four continents.

Her work is characterized by a meticulous approach to natural light, an intuitive sense of composition, and a belief that every frame should tell a story. From the stark deserts of Namibia to the neon-soaked streets of Tokyo, Lena's camera finds beauty in the in-between moments.

When not on set, she mentors emerging filmmakers and curates visual essays exploring the intersection of cinema and memory.
