Sourced Worldwide

Precious Ingredients

01
Bulgarian Rose Absolute
Valley of Roses, Bulgaria

Hand-harvested at dawn during the brief May bloom. It takes 3,500 kilograms of rose petals to produce one kilogram of absolute, making it one of the world's most precious natural materials.

🌹
02
Laotian Oud
Attapeu Province, Laos

Sustainably harvested from Aquilaria trees after decades of natural resin formation. Each piece is selected by master graders for its distinctive honeyed, animalic character.

🪵
03
Madagascan Vanilla
Sava Region, Madagascar

Cured for nine months using traditional Bourbon method. The resulting pods yield a rich, creamy absolute with notes of caramel, dried fruit, and warm spice.

🌿
04
Indian Sandalwood
Mysore, Karnataka, India

From sustainably managed plantations, this sandalwood requires 30 years of growth before harvest. Its creamy, milky warmth forms the soul of our most iconic compositions.
