Our Story

Born from the
Mediterranean

Each Soléil piece is conceived on the sun-drenched shores of the Italian Riviera, where our founder first dreamt of swimwear that felt as beautiful as the landscape itself.

"We don't design for the beach — we design for the woman who becomes the most radiant thing on it."

Our atelier in Positano employs artisan techniques passed through three generations, combining heritage craftsmanship with innovative sustainable fabrics that honor the ocean we call home.

For the most accurate fit, we recommend measuring yourself with a soft tape measure over bare skin or lightweight clothing.

Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape level.

Waist: Measure at the narrowest part of your natural waistline.

Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.

Between sizes? We recommend sizing up for a relaxed resort fit, or down for a sleeker silhouette.

To preserve the beauty and longevity of your Soléil pieces:

• Hand wash in cool water with mild detergent after each wear

• Lay flat to dry in shade — avoid wringing or tumble drying

• Rinse in fresh water after exposure to chlorine or salt water

• Avoid rough surfaces that may snag delicate fabrics

• Store flat or folded; never hang swimwear as it may stretch
