Our Philosophy

Games Are the Art Form of Our Time

We believe interactive experiences have the power to move people in ways no other medium can. Every game we create starts with a question: what feeling do we want to leave with the player?

Founded in 2018 by a team of industry veterans from studios like Naughty Dog, CD Projekt, and FromSoftware, NexusForge has grown from 5 passionate creators to a 200-person studio united by one mission.
